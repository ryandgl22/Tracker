Your winter plan

Nutrition: ~2,200 kcal on normal days and ~170 g protein. Keep planned treats if they fit. Measure easy-to-underestimate extras such as cheese, peanut butter, oil, spreads and sauces.

Strength: Full Body A Monday/Thursday; Full Body B Tuesday/Friday; Wednesday recovery. Use double progression and generally finish working sets with 1–3 reps in reserve.

Cycling: Weekend cycling/cardio. Normal ~1-hour winter sessions broadly stay on the normal plan; genuinely long/hard rides should be fuelled appropriately.

Recovery: If a big Saturday ride leaves heavy fatigue on Monday, reduce leg work or use a recovery day. Cycling remains the main sporting priority.

Progress: Use 7-day weight averages, waist measurements every 1–2 weeks and progress photos around every 4 weeks. Review calories after 3–4 genuinely consistent weeks rather than reacting to individual weigh-ins.

Backup & Transfer

This version stores your day-to-day entries on the device you're using. The backup file is what makes the tracker portable between your iPhone and laptop.

Last backup: never

Suggested workflow: mainly log on your phone → export a backup to Files/iCloud Drive/OneDrive → import it on your laptop when you want the larger analysis view. Import replaces the current device's dataset, so export the newer device first.

Browser/site data can be cleared, so the backup file is your durable copy. Keep a recent backup somewhere outside the browser.
